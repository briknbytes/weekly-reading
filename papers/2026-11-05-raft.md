# In Search of an Understandable Consensus Algorithm (Raft)

🔗 [Read it here](https://raft.github.io/raft.pdf) · Diego Ongaro & John Ousterhout, Stanford (USENIX ATC 2014, extended version)

## Why read it

Raft is the consensus algorithm behind etcd, and so behind every Kubernetes cluster. It was designed above all to be understandable, unlike Paxos. Sections 1–5 are the core; 6–8 cover membership changes, snapshots and clients.

Play with it first: https://raft.github.io (interactive demo on the page).

## Questions to think about

- Why do election timeouts need to be random?
- Why can't a new leader commit entries from an older term just by counting replicas? (Figure 8)
- What actually happens to a Kubernetes cluster when etcd loses its leader?
